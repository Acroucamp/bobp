**Full description (max 4000):**
[APP NAME] connects people who need a service with the businesses that provide it.

FOR CUSTOMERS
- Browse businesses and their services, with clear prices and durations
- Pick a day and a time that is actually free
- Track your bookings and cancel when plans change

FOR SERVICE PROVIDERS
- Set up your business and list your services in minutes
- Confirm, decline and complete bookings
- See today's appointments, bookings and revenue at a glance
- Search and filter appointments by status, date or customer

Your bookings are protected by account-level access rules: customers only see their own
bookings, and businesses only see their own. You can delete your account at any time in the app.
