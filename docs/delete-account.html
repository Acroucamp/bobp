Delete your Appointly account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email support@avenlume.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, account type) and your sign-in details.
  • Customers: businesses you booked with keep a record of the booking, without your name.
  • Service providers: your businesses, their services and all of their bookings are deleted.

See our privacy policy for more.
