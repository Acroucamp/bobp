Lekkerbook

Bookings, made lekker.

Lekkerbook is a free Android app for small service businesses and the people who book them. Providers list their services and see their day; customers pick a time and book in a few taps.

For providers

Add your business and services, set who works when, confirm or complete bookings, and see your bookings and revenue on a simple dashboard.

For customers

Browse businesses, see real free time slots, book, and keep track of your appointments in one place.
